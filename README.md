I'm a full-stack developer with about five years of experience. Most of what I build runs in public
spaces: the screens and audio systems that people rely on in metro stations and airports. I work
with microservices, Domain-Driven Design and RESTful APIs that keep multi-platform applications in sync.

---

## What I work on

- **São Paulo Metro, Line 17 (Gold).** I receive real-time data for every train in operation from BYD's
  system, process it and show arrival times and destinations on the platform monitors. It is running in
  the stations today.
- **Guarulhos International Airport (GRU).** Flight information screens fed by the airport's API, and an
  SNMP MIB tree to monitor the devices in the field.
- **Public address audio.** A gateway that turns SIP calls and multicast RTP into Dante network audio,
  with automatic gain control that adapts to the noise of the station.
- **Signage apps.** React Native apps for Android TV and Samsung displays, C++ SIP services integrated
  with Node.js, and .NET desktop tools.
- **Before that, at BMS Tecnologia.** Energy contract management for factories (meter readings,
  consumption over the contract period, tariffs), built with Laravel on AWS, gathering requirements
  directly with the client.
